WHY CONTROLLED CHAOS?

In a world dominated by complex JavaScript frameworks and libraries, Controlled Chaos stands as a testament to the power of simplicity. This website is built entirely with HTML and CSS, showcasing how creativity and innovation can thrive even within constraints.

By embracing the limitations of not using JavaScript, we unlock new possibilities for design and interaction. The animations, illusions, and deliberate disorder you see here are all achieved through clever use of CSS techniques.

We invite you to explore, interact, and experience the beauty of Controlled Chaos. Let it inspire you to think differently about web development and the endless potential of HTML and CSS.

As a member of the web development community, I created this project to challenge conventional norms and demonstrate that even without JavaScript, we can create engaging and dynamic web experiences.

Generate AI is begining to become an old topic now. However, the crisis of job loss is growing as AI takes over more tasks. Although AI can be useful in web developement, I created this project to embrace the fundamentals that we humans have created, and to showcase the creativity that can be achieved without the user of AI tools. So join me now, let's create something that is truly ours, without the help of AI. AI dossen't bring us up, it pulls us down. Let's cut the dead weight and embrace what is our own, buetiful, creativity.
